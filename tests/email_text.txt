08:29:21  TOMMY HO : US IG Banks – Morning Brief: Risk-Off Tone Returns; Flows Turn Defensive as Geopolitics Weigh

Market Snapshot:
A risk off tone dominated Wednesday as fresh Middle East hostilities rattled markets. Iran launched a barrage of ballistic missiles and drones, disrupting regional infrastructure and pushing oil higher. Equities sold off, with the Dow -621pts and the S&P -0.7%. Financials also came under pressure following stress in private markets, with redemption limits triggering broader weakness across alternatives and bank equities. On the AI front, Alphabet successfully upsized its $80bn equity raise despite closing -0.8% on the day. UST yields moved higher, with the 10yr at 4.50% (+4bp).

Credit Spread:
Spreads widened in line with the risk off tone, with the Bloomberg US IG Corporate Index at Z+73.6 (+0.8bp). Technology underperformed (+1.7bp), reversing prior tightening, while Communications (+1.2bp) and Consumer Cyclical (+1.0bp) also lagged. Bank senior and subordinated moved in lockstep (+0.8bp to 66.8bp and 101.6bp), with financials broadly pressured by risk sentiment and private credit contagion concerns. A sizeable extension trade helped limit further widening, highlighting ongoing demand for duration at higher all in yields.

Electronic Flow Dynamics:
Flows tilted defensive with better selling across Senior and T2, concentrated in GSIBs and European financials. In Senior, pressure was focused in the 5–10y belly and intermediate FRNs, while activity further out the curve was more balanced with selective demand. T2 also leaned toward better selling, particularly in longer dated structures, although intermittent buying continued to emerge in higher quality and longer duration exposures.

US IG Issuance:
Primary activity slowed sharply, with just four deals pricing $3.2bn following recent heavy supply. WTD issuance stands at $40.4bn, already exceeding dealer forecasts, with YTD supply at $1,054.3bn. Yankee bank issuance continues to dominate, accounting for more than $24bn over the past two weeks, or roughly one third of total supply over that period.
